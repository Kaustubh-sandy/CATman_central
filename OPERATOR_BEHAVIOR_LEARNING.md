# Operator Behavior & Personalized Learning Engine

> **Subsystem**: CAT Smart Operator Companion — Behavior & Personalized Learning  
> **Architecture Style**: 100% Deterministic, Explainable, Closed-Loop Telematics  
> **No Black-Box Machine Learning**: Fully auditable mathematical equations and telemetry thresholds.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Philosophy

Traditional fleet management systems grade heavy equipment operators with opaque, aggregated scores (e.g., "Score: 71/100") generated by black-box algorithms or distant machine learning models. This approach creates severe operational friction:
* **Lack of Trust & Transparency**: Neither the operator nor the supervisor can explain why points were docked or which specific actions caused the penalty.
* **Open-Loop Disconnect**: Telematics monitoring is severed from training. When bad habits are flagged, operators are rarely provided immediate, actionable, or contextual learning opportunities.
* **Unfair Penalties**: Generic fleet algorithms fail to account for operational wait times (e.g., waiting for haul trucks in a loading queue) or site environmental extremes (e.g., monsoon mud or scorching heat).

The **CAT Smart Operator Companion Behavior Engine** solves this by implementing an **explainable, deterministic, and closed-loop behavior analysis pipeline**:
1. **Mathematical Baselines**: Uses an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) across 5 core real-world skills.
2. **Context-Aware Filtering**: Automatically relaxes thresholds or discounts idle time during legitimate wait states, rain, or high ambient heat.
3. **Closed Learning Loop**: When persistent weaknesses or safety patterns are identified, the system automatically assigns interactive **3D cab simulations** and tracks subsequent real-world shifts to verify and reward measurable improvement.

## 3. The 5 Core Skill Dimensions & Mathematical Formulas

Every skill starts with a maximum raw score of **100 points**. Penalties are deducted based on normalized deviation beyond a calibrated threshold.

```
Deviation = max(0, ObservedMetric - (Threshold × ContextMultiplier))
NormalizedDeviation = Deviation / max(BaselineValue, Threshold, 0.01)
Penalty = clamp(round(NormalizedDeviation × Weight), 0, Cap)
Score = clamp(round(100 - Σ Penalties + Bonuses), 0, 100)
```

### Skill 1: Idle Management (`IDLE_MANAGEMENT`)
Targeted at eliminating unnecessary fuel burn and engine wear without penalizing operational waiting.
* **Metrics Tracked**:
  - `idleRatio`: Total shift idle minutes divided by total shift duration.
  - `unjustifiedIdleRatio`: Idle time accrued while the machine is not paused and not in an active loading/unloading cycle.
* **Bonus**: $+5$ points awarded if shift idle ratio is below $8\%$.
* **Linked Simulation**: `SIM_SHUTDOWN` (End-of-shift shutdown and idle reduction).

### Skill 2: Fuel Efficiency (`FUEL_EFFICIENCY`)
Evaluates fuel consumption normalized by productive work output.
* **Metrics Tracked**:
  - `fuelPerCycleRatio`: Litres of fuel consumed per completed truck loading cycle ($\Delta \text{fuel} / \text{cyclesDone}$).
  - `idleFuelRatio`: Estimated fuel burned while idling ($\approx 4.0\text{ L/h}$) divided by total shift fuel burn.
* **Linked Simulation**: `SIM_SHUTDOWN`.

### Skill 3: Smooth Operation (`SMOOTH_OPERATION`)
Measures machine mechanical sympathy, hydraulic handling, and wear reduction.
* **Metrics Tracked**:
  - `rpmVarianceRatio`: Standard deviation of `engineRpm` during `OPERATING` states.
  - `vibrationRatio`: Fraction of operating time where structural vibration exceeds $0.6\text{g}$.
  - `throttleChopRate`: Frequency of rapid directional reversals in throttle pedal position per minute.
  - `impactRate`: Sudden shock load events ($> 2.5\text{g}$) per shift hour.
* **Linked Simulation**: `SIM_OVERHEAT` / `SIM_STARTUP`.

### Skill 4: Safety Awareness (`SAFETY_AWARENESS`)
Audits adherence to life-safety operating envelopes.
* **Metrics Tracked**:
  - `safetyEventsPerHour`: Rate of generated safety alerts per operating hour.
  - `seatbeltViolations`: Absolute count of unbelted operation incidents.
  - `proximityEvents`: Breaches of dynamic swing-radius proximity zones.
* **Linked Simulation**: `SIM_PROXIMITY_RAIN` (Hazard avoidance and worker safety).

### Skill 5: Task Execution (`TASK_EXECUTION`)
Evaluates cycle consistency and production timeline predictability.
* **Metrics Tracked**:
  - `taskTimeOverrun`: Mean ratio of $(\text{actualMin} / \text{predictedMin}) - 1$.
  - `taskCompletionRate`: Ratio of successfully completed tasks versus assigned tasks.
  - `cycleRateVariance`: Coefficient of variation in load cycles per operating minute.
* **Linked Simulation**: `SIM_STARTUP`.

## 4. The 4-Layer Context Filter

Before telemetry is charged to an operator's record, it is processed through a multi-layer context filter to guarantee fairness:

1. **Operational State Gating**:
   - Idle time accrued while in `LOADING` or `UNLOADING` state with parking brake set is classified as operational queue wait time and excluded from unjustified idle deductions.
   - RPM variance and throttle chop are only evaluated during working states (`OPERATING`, `TRANSPORTING`), ignoring machine warm-up.
2. **Site & Environmental Relaxation Factors**:
   - When the site condition is `RAIN`: Thresholds for idle ratio and cycle time are relaxed by **30%** ($\times 1.3$).
   - When site visibility is `LOW`: Speed and RPM thresholds are relaxed by **20%** ($\times 1.2$).
   - When ambient temperature exceeds $38^\circ\text{C}$: Overheating and engine warm-up thresholds are discounted by **20%** ($\times 1.2$).
3. **Task Pause Protection**:
   - Time spent in `PAUSED` task state (e.g. shift briefing, blasting delays) is automatically subtracted from shift idle time.
4. **Minimum Observation Duration**:
   - Shifts under **10 minutes** of active duration are excluded from the ledger to prevent test drives and warmups from polluting the baseline.

## 5. Operator Baselines & Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

Rather than benchmarking operators against arbitrary fleet averages, each operator builds a **personalized baseline**.

* **Baseline Establishment**: An operator's baseline is established after their first **3 completed shifts** (`MIN_SHIFTS_FOR_BASELINE = 3`).
* **Standard Shift Update ($\alpha = 0.2$)**:
  $$\text{Baseline}_{\text{new}} = 0.2 \cdot \text{Observed}_{\text{shift}} + 0.8 \cdot \text{Baseline}_{\text{prior}}$$
  This requires $8\text{--}10$ consistent shifts to permanently alter the baseline, insulating the operator against one-off bad days.
* **Accelerated Post-Training Update ($\alpha = 0.5$)**:
  When an operator proves significant real-world improvement following a simulation module, an accelerated learning rate ($\alpha = 0.5$) is applied once to give immediate credit for adopted best practices.

## 6. Dynamic Recommendation Engine

At the conclusion of every shift, the behavior engine evaluates recommendations using three priority triggers:

| Trigger Code | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `SKILL_SCORE_LOW` | Skill score $< 55$ points | Recommends corresponding simulation module. |
| `SKILL_DECLINING` | Skill score trending downward for 3 consecutive shifts | Recommends corresponding refresher simulation. |
| `SAFETY_REPEAT` | Same safety `ruleId` triggered in $\ge 3$ of the last 5 shifts | High-urgency recommendation mapped via rule matrix. |

* **Deduplication Rule**: If an operator passed a recommended simulation within the last **14 days**, re-recommendation is suppressed to avoid training fatigue.

## 7. The Closed Learning Loop

```
1. PRE-TRAINING SNAPSHOT
   └─ Engine flags: Idle Management = 52 (NEEDS_ATTENTION)
   └─ Stores snapshot: { score: 52, idleRatio: 0.26 }

2. TARGETED ASSIGNMENT
   └─ E-Learning surfaces SIM_SHUTDOWN with badge:
      "Idle Management score is 52 — below 55"

3. 3D CAB SIMULATION
   └─ Operator completes virtual cab drill (Three.js)
   └─ onTrainingComplete() records score (92/100, passed)
   └─ Opens 14-day tracking window on behavior ledger

4. POST-TRAINING RE-EVALUATION
   └─ Operator works next 3–4 real-world shifts
   └─ Engine re-evaluates Idle Management score
   └─ New Score = 76 (+24 pts improvement!)

5. LOOP CLOSED & REWARDED
   └─ Δ >= 10 pts threshold met (improved: true)
   └─ Emits Socket.IO 'behavior:loop_closed'
   └─ Displays animated celebration banner in cab UI
   └─ Applies accelerated baseline update (alpha = 0.5)
```
